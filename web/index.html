REVIEW DETAILS

Bring your reviews into focus.

Import a CSV into My workspace. Existing reviews stay safe, and duplicates are skipped automatically.

What should my CSV contain?

Use these column headers. Review IDs can be empty to generate a stable ID.

asin, product_url, review_id, reviewer_name, verified_purchase, review_title, rating, review_date, review_body

Product URLs must use HTTPS on Amazon India and match the ASIN. Ratings can be empty or between 1 and 5. Use true, false, or empty for verified_purchase.

Your CSV stays in this local workspace.
